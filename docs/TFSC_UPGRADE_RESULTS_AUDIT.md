# TFSC UPGRADE RESULTS AUDIT

Answers to the ten mandated questions, computed from
`data/processed/sim_trajectories.csv`, `sim_regime_map.csv`,
`sim_adaptation.csv`, `sim_hysteresis.csv`, `sim_pathdep.csv`,
`sim_r3_window.csv` (generated by `analysis/run_transition_sims.py`,
seed-independent closed-form; viability thresholds TH_LOSS=0.05V,
TH_VER=0.20V, TH_ISS=0.30V, α=0.05, κ_floor=1.5, burden_coef=0.5).

## 1. Does an endogenous regime transition exist?

Yes. Under myopic (Rule A) and forward-looking (Rule C, β=0.9, H=40) issuer
behavior — identical sequences — the optimal regime changes endogenously as
the technology state θ_t moves:

- T1 (symmetric progress): R1 → R2 → R4 (first transition step 24)
- T2 (attacker-favoring): R1 → R2 → R3 → R4 (first transition step 18)
- T3 (defender-favoring): R1 persists over the full horizon — no transition
- T4 (discontinuous synthesis breakthrough): R1 → R4 in one step at step 20

## 2. What are the dominant transition sequences?

Fixed → rotating → exit (T1), and fixed → rotating → lineage-preserving →
exit (T2). Defender-favoring progress produces no transition (fixed
provenance never loses viability when adversarial synthesis stays expensive).
A breakthrough produces direct fixed → exit (rotation cannot respond to a
discontinuous cost drop; there is no intermediate regime to occupy).

## 3. Is physical lineage ever optimal?

Yes, but only in a **narrow technology window**: on T2, R3 is endogenously
optimal for exactly 2 of 40 progression steps (steps 26–27) — when bulk
synthesis is already cheap enough to kill rotating provenance (3·c_syn·(1−q)
< V) but microfabrication remains expensive enough that the lineage floor
(ακ·c_micro) keeps forging unprofitable. The window widens slightly under a
weaker attacker (d_eff_penalty=0.5 → 3 steps) and shrinks under stronger
geometry (penalty=2 → 1 step). On the (c_syn, c_ver) regime map the window
is thinner than grid resolution at all three c_micro tiers — no visible R3
region. Verdict: physical lineage is optimal only in a transient, narrow
corridor; it is a delaying architecture, not a destination.

## 4. Does bulk chaining remain dominated?

Yes — it does not appear as a candidate regime at all (M3a proven null under
the synthetic attacker in Phase 4); the model correctly contains no
"successful bulk chaining" regime. The null is now theoretically reframed:
physical inheritance without persistent, structure-preserved predecessor
information is not institutional memory.

## 5. How large is adaptation value?

vs the best fixed provenance architecture (per 40-step horizon, V-units):

- T1: Δ_adapt = 1.24 (κ=0), decaying to 0.74 at κ=0.5; adaptive switching
  extends provenance viability by +27.5% of the horizon.
- T2: Δ_adapt = −0.53 — adaptive fighting costs more than immediate exit
  (L_adaptive > L_exit: −0.65). Under attacker-favoring progress the optimal
  "adaptation" is early abandonment, not succession of regimes.
- T3: Δ_adapt = 0 — nothing to adapt to.
- T4: Δ_adapt = 0 vs best fixed; −0.05 vs immediate exit.

Adaptation value is therefore **trajectory-dependent, not universal**: it is
positive under symmetric progress, zero under defender-favoring progress,
and negative under attacker-favoring or breakthrough progress where exit is
the rational response.

## 6. Does switching cost create hysteresis?

No. κ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1} produces identical transition steps and
sequences on every trajectory (sim_hysteresis.csv). The reason is
structural, not parametric: all observed transitions are *viability-forced*
(incumbent crosses a hard viability constraint E[L_F] < 0.05V), not
J-margin switches where switching cost could delay a voluntary move.
Hysteresis would require a region where two regimes are simultaneously
viable and the incumbent survives on switching cost alone; the model
contains no such overlap at tested resolutions. Path-dependence test: two
paths (fast T2 vs slow ramp) ending at the same θ_T give identical endpoint
regimes at all κ — no path dependence found.

## 7. Does c_syn/V ≈ 0.05 remain important?

Partially superseded. In the static phase diagram 0.05 was the collapse
frontier. Under endogenous adaptation the operative boundaries are the
per-regime expiration lines: fixed dies at c_syn ≈ V (1), rotating at
c_syn ≈ V/3 (0.33), lineage at 3c_syn + 0.075c_micro ≈ V. The 0.05 frontier
survives as the *deep-obsolescence* region where every provenance regime —
including lineage — has exited; i.e., it approximates the R4 boundary for
the last-surviving architecture under cheap microfabrication, not the
onset of vulnerability. The frontier is now a stack of expiration surfaces,
not a single threshold.

## 8. What does d_eff collapse do to transition timing?

Lower effective dimensionality moves every expiration surface earlier:
parameterized as a divisor on attacker cost (d_eff_penalty), stronger
geometry (penalty 2) shortens the T2 lineage window from 2 steps to 1,
weaker (0.5) extends it to 3. Correlated markers thus *accelerate* the whole
institutional sequence — transition timing is d_eff-dependent.

## 9. What does coalition-size reversal do to optimal regime choice?

From Phase 4 (unchanged result): larger federations concentrate attack
mixtures near the centroid, easing reconstruction — i.e., N enters the
attacker-cost term. In the dynamic model it shifts the same expiration
surfaces; it does not change the *ordering* of regimes, only their position
in technology space. Reported as a shift parameter, not a regime-changer.

## 10. Which results are genuinely relevant to TFSC?

- A general mechanism: technology changes *which* authentication
  institution is optimal, not merely its security level (Proposition B).
- Endogenous transition sequences with an explicit technological
  expiration frontier (T1: fixed→rotating→exit).
- A conditional-positive verdict on physical lineage: optimal only in a
  narrow, quantified window — a boundary result, not advocacy.
- Trajectory-dependence of adaptation value: adaptation helps under
  symmetric progress and hurts under attacker-favoring progress where
  early exit dominates.
- Structural absence of hysteresis (viability-forced transitions) and
  path independence — honest nulls that delimit where institutional
  switching-cost theory does and does not bind.
- Correlated-marker collapse accelerating institutional expiration.
